# Product positioning statement

*88 words.*

Every other assistant remembers what you told it. Kivi remembers what you told everyone
else: the deadline you gave your team, the promise you made a partner, the decision you
wrote down late at night.

Kivi keeps all of it searchable, promoting only what you are likely to need again. Dates,
owners, decisions, numbers, and how you asked something to be written. Every memory stays
connected to the words it came from.

Ask Hey Kivi anything and it answers from your own dictations, shows you what it used, or
says you never said it.

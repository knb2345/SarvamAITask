# Product positioning statement

*93 words.*

Every other assistant remembers what you told it. Kivi remembers what you told everyone
else: the deadline you gave your team, the promise you made a partner, or the decision you
wrote down late at night.

Kivi keeps it all searchable, but only makes memories of things you are likely to need
again: dates, owners, decisions, numbers, and how you asked something to be written.
Each memory links back to your words.

Ask Hey Kivi anything. It answers from your dictations, shows what it used, or tells you
that you never said it.

# Product positioning statement

*97 words.*

Every other assistant remembers what you tell it. Kivi remembers what you told everyone else:
the deadline you gave your team, the promise you made a partner, the decision you wrote
down late at night.

Everything you dictated stays searchable. Kivi turns only what you are likely to need again
into memory: dates, owners, decisions, numbers, and how you asked something to be written.
Each memory stays connected to the words it came from.

Ask Hey Kivi anything. It answers from your own dictations, shows you what it used, or tells
you when you never said it.

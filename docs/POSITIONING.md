# Product positioning statement

*97 words.*

Every other assistant remembers what you asked it. Kivi hears what you told everyone
else — the deadline you gave your team, the promise you made a partner, the decision you
wrote up at midnight.

Kivi keeps all of it searchable, and promotes only the few things you would otherwise
have to look up again: dates, owners, decisions, numbers, and how you asked for things to
be written. Each keeps the words that produced it.

Ask Hey Kivi anything and it answers from your own dictations, showing you which — or
tells you that you never said it.
